Every women-led application follows the same five steps.

No case skips a stage, and no single person can move a large loan through on their own. The amount decides how far up the chain a case has to travel, with a focus on supporting rural women borrowers and women-led enterprises.

01

Application logged

Field team / HOI

Input

Applicant name, village, purpose of the loan, amount requested.

Output

A new case enters the system with status "Submitted."

02

Ground verification

Head of Institution

Input

Identity documents, land or asset records, repayment history in the village.

Output

Recommend the case forward to the Director, or reject it outright with a remark.

03

Risk review

Director

Input

The HOI's recommendation, the requested amount, and current portfolio exposure.

Output

Up to ₹2,00,000: approve or reject directly. Above ₹2,00,000: escalate to the Founder, or reject.

04

Final sign-off on larger cases

Founder

Input

The full case file, the Director's assessment, and Foundation-wide lending policy.

Output

Final approval or rejection — there is no stage above this one.

05

Disbursement

System

Input

A final "approved" decision from whichever role had authority to give one.

Output

Funds released to the applicant; the case is marked "Disbursed" and closed.

Why the ₹2,00,000 line exists

Most rural credit needs — a milch animal, farm inputs, a small trade stock top-up — fall well under this line, so the Director can move quickly without waiting on the Founder's calendar. Larger requests carry more portfolio risk, so they get a second, more senior set of eyes before money moves. The threshold is a policy setting, not a fixed rule — the Founder can adjust it at any time.
