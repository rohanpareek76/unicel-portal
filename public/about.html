Built for women who are ready to grow.

UNICEL Rural Creditcare Foundation was started to close a simple gap: rural women with real repayment capacity but no paperwork trail that a bank recognises. We built our own trail — one based on presence in the village, not just documents in a file. What began as a handful of small loans to a few dozen families has grown into a structured lending institution, but the founding idea has never changed: trust is built on the ground, one household at a time, long before it is written into a ledger.

We work with families who are often invisible to formal finance — not because they are unreliable, but because the system was never designed to see them. A woman running a dairy, a stitching unit, or a small vegetable stall may have years of consistent income and a spotless repayment history within her own community, yet no credit score to show for it. Our field process exists to translate that lived reliability into something a lending decision can actually be based on.

Our approach

We lend in small amounts, close to home, with a review chain short enough to move in days rather than months — and accountable enough that no single person disburses a large loan alone. Every application passes through ground verification, a risk-aware review, and — where the amount warrants it — a final check from the top, so speed never comes at the cost of care.

We also believe transparency shouldn't stop at disbursal. Every decision, whether an approval or a rejection, is recorded with the reason behind it, so the process stays honest and easy to explain — to the borrower, to our team, and to anyone auditing our work.

Where we work

Currently active across villages in and around Jaipur district, Rajasthan, with field verification carried out in person by the Head of Institution's team. We are gradually expanding into neighbouring districts as our field capacity grows, always prioritising depth of relationship in each village over speed of expansion.

Our long-term goal is simple: to become the lender rural women think of first — not because we are the only option, but because we are the fairest one.
