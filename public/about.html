Built for women who are ready to grow.

UNICEL Rural Creditcare Foundation was started to close a simple gap: rural women with real repayment capacity but no paperwork trail that a bank recognises. We built our own trail — one based on presence in the village, not just documents in a file.

Leadership

Our approach

We lend in small amounts, close to home, with a review chain short enough to move in days rather than months — and accountable enough that no single person disburses a large loan alone.

Where we work

Currently active across villages in and around Jaipur district, Rajasthan, with field verification carried out in person by the Head of Institution's team.
