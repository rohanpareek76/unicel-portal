Small loans deserve careful eyes, not slow ones.

Every application is verified on the ground, assessed for risk, and — for larger amounts — signed off by the Foundation's leadership before a rupee moves. Nothing sits in a queue longer than it needs to.

Ground verification

Our Head of Institution and field team confirm identity, land records and repayment history before anything is recommended upward.

Risk-aware approval

The Director weighs each case against portfolio risk, approving directly within policy limits.

Founder oversight

Larger or unusual cases reach the Founder directly, keeping the Foundation's judgment close to every big decision.
